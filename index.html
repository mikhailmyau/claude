About & philosophy

est. 2017

The best interfaces are felt before they are understood. I obsess over the half-second — easing curves, hover states, the weight of a page as it loads — because craft at that scale is what turns a website into an experience worth remembering.

№ 1

Motion with meaning

Animation is choreography, not decoration. Every transition should explain where you are and where you're going.

№ 2

Performance is design

A beautiful site that stutters isn't beautiful. I budget every frame — 60fps is part of the aesthetic.

№ 3

Details compound

Cursor states, focus rings, scroll physics, empty states. A hundred small decisions are the whole product.
